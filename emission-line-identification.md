## 3. Spectral Identification Criteria

The reported spectral lines were evaluated based on:

- Wavelength agreement within ±0.65 nm of reference values.
- Signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) greater than 3.
- Relatively high intensity within the corresponding spectral region.

## 4. Interpretation of Relative Intensity

Relative intensity was treated as a qualitative descriptor of the apparent strength of emission lines in the recorded spectrum.

Since the NIST Atomic Spectra Database does not establish the origin and experimental conditions of the relative intensity values in the measured spectrum, these values were not interpreted as direct quantitative measurements.
