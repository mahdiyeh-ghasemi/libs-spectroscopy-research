
# Experimental Parameters

## Laser Specifications

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Laser source | Nd:YAG |
| Wavelength | 1064 nm |
| Pulse duration | 6 ns |
| Pulse energy | Approximately 10–100 mJ |

## Experimental Technique

Laser-Induced Breakdown Spectroscopy (LIBS)

## Optical Detection

- Plasma emission collection
- Optical fiber transmission
- Spectrometer-based detection
- Time-controlled measurements using a delay generator

## Material Investigated

Cu–Al2O3 composite materials

## Research Focus

- Plasma emission spectroscopy
- Elemental emission line identification
- Spectral analysis
- Self-absorption effects
