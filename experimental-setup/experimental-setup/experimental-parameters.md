# Experimental Parameters and Measurement Conditions

## 1. Laser Specifications

| Parameter | Experimental Value |
|---|---|
| Laser source | Q-switched Nd:YAG |
| Wavelength | 1064 nm |
| Pulse energy | 26.1 mJ |
| Repetition rate | 1 Hz |
| Pulse duration | 5 ms |

## 2. Sample Positioning and Focusing

- The sample was mounted on an adjustable three-axis (XYZ) micrometric stage.
- A converging lens with a focal length of 150 mm was used to focus the laser beam onto the sample surface and establish the appropriate energy density.

## 3. Optical Detection and Acquisition

- Plasma emission was collected and transferred through an optical fiber.
- A spectrometer with a spectral resolution of 0.44 nm was used for optical emission measurements.
- A delay generator was used to control the detection delay.
- The detection delay was set to 1.5 μs.
- Signal monitoring and experimental measurements involved an oscilloscope and computer-based data acquisition.

## 4. Material Investigated

Cu–Al2O3 composite materials.

An investigated sample contained 1.1 wt% Al2O3.

## 5. Spectroscopic Analysis

- Identification of characteristic copper and aluminum emission lines.
- Comparison with reference spectral data, including the NIST Atomic Spectra Database.
- Evaluation of relative emission intensity.
- Signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) greater than 3 for reported spectral lines.
- Wavelength agreement within ±0.65 nm of reference values.
- Investigation of self-absorption effects.

## 6. Research Focus

The experimental measurements focused on laser-induced plasma emission characteristics and the influence of self-absorption on quantitative LIBS analysis.
