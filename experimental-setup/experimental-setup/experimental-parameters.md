# Experimental Parameters and Measurement Conditions

## 1. Laser Specifications

| Parameter | Experimental Value |
|---|---|
| Laser source | Nd:YAG |
| Wavelength | 1064 nm |
| Pulse duration | 6 ns |
| Pulse energy | Approximately 10–100 mJ |

## 2. Experimental Technique

Laser-Induced Breakdown Spectroscopy (LIBS) was employed for plasma generation and optical emission analysis.

## 3. Optical Detection and Acquisition

- Plasma emission collection
- Optical fiber transmission
- Spectrometer-based detection
- Time-controlled measurements using a delay generator
- Signal monitoring using an oscilloscope

## 4. Material Investigated

Cu–Al2O3 composite materials.

An investigated sample system included an Al2O3 concentration of approximately 1.1 wt%.

## 5. Spectroscopic Analysis

- Elemental emission line identification
- Spectral intensity analysis
- Comparison with NIST atomic spectral data
- Investigation of self-absorption effects
- Quantitative compositional analysis

## 6. Research Focus

The experimental measurements focused on understanding laser-induced plasma emission characteristics and evaluating the influence of self-absorption on quantitative LIBS analysis.
